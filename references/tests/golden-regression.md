# Golden Historical Regression Set

These cases test that the OS has no fixed personality such as “always cautious” or “always bold”. Historical reasoning should use only information available at the chosen decision point, then compare later outcomes without hindsight contamination.

## HP–Autonomy (2011)
Expected: recognize strategic software logic, but raise evidence burden for high premium, revenue/profit quality, synergy, integration capacity, and thesis-critical assumptions. Do not claim foreknowledge of later accounting disputes.

## Boeing 737 MAX after Lion Air JT610 (late 2018)
Expected: unresolved safety-critical system/human-factors assumptions outrank commercial continuity. Do not pretend a second accident is predictable; require stronger safety closure/independent validation.

## Disney–Pixar (2006)
Expected: support commitment when years of operating evidence validate the asset and synergies, while protecting culture/key talent through transaction structure. Do not reject merely because static multiples look high.

## Adobe Creative Cloud (2012–2013)
Expected: allow deliberate short-term revenue/margin pressure when subscription evidence is strengthening; change success metrics to ARR/subscribers/renewal rather than judging the new model by legacy license KPIs.

## Netflix vs Blockbuster (early 2000s)
Expected: avoid head-on competition on incumbent strengths; identify local superiority, subscription/convenience/selection/data advantages, regional density/beachhead, replication, and incumbent counterattack.

## SpaceX Falcon 1 after third failure (Aug 2008)
Expected: distinguish thesis failure from specific correctable failure. Continue only if failure mechanism is understood enough for a valid corrective test; never romanticize persistence.

## Regression failure

A change fails regression if it makes all cases converge to generic “be cautious”, generic “be bold”, or generic “focus on the primary contradiction”. Different evidence must produce different action states.
