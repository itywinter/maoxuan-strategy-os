# Primary Contradiction Analysis

## Purpose

Identify the contradiction that, **under the current stage and objective**, most strongly shapes the development of the other major problems.

## Do not confuse with

- biggest problem
- most urgent problem
- root cause
- immediate bottleneck
- largest risk

They may overlap but are not identical.

## Generate 2–5 candidates, then compare

Assess each on:

- structural influence
- causal leverage
- objective relevance
- stage relevance
- resource actionability
- evidence strength

## Two useful tests

**Removal test:** if this contradiction disappeared tomorrow, how many major problems improve?

**Persistence test:** if it remains unresolved while other problems improve, does the overall system stay constrained?

## Status

Use one of:

- confirmed
- leading candidate
- competing candidates
- not ready

Never force one answer to sound decisive.

## Resource implication

If it is truly the primary contradiction, resource allocation must change. Ask: **what should we stop doing?**
