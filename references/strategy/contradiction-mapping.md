# Contradiction Mapping

## Purpose

Turn a flat issue list into relationships: cause, constraint, dependency, reinforcement, balancing loop, trade-off, symptom, mediator.

## Workflow

1. Convert vague labels into variables that can change.
2. Map directional relationships.
3. For every important arrow record mechanism, timing, alternative explanations, and evidence strength.
4. Separate symptoms from structural drivers.
5. Identify reinforcing/balancing loops and meaningful trade-offs.
6. Express real tensions as two-sided contradictions only when the two sides genuinely interact.

Example:

`unclear scope → unclear incremental value → weak business case → weak budget priority → no procurement action`

Do not mistake a clean diagram for proven causality; mark weak arrows as hypotheses.

## Stakeholder motive

An actor's action may reduce momentum without proving the actor intended to block the project. Separate observed effect from inferred motive.
