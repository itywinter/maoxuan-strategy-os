# Strategic Focus & Resource Concentration

## Purpose

Turn a strategic choice into a real center of effort.

## Core rule

A priority is rhetorical until resources move.

Define a **breakthrough point** that is specific, stage-relevant, measurable, evidence-producing, and achievable with current resources.

## Resource inventory

Consider scarce:

- best people
- cash/capital
- management attention
- time window
- technology
- customer/partner access
- organizational capacity

## Required output

- main effort
- supporting efforts
- owner and decision rights
- resource allocation
- do now
- maintain minimally
- delay
- stop
- time window
- success/failure/exit criteria

## Integrity test

Check the calendar, budget, strongest talent, and stopped work. If they do not reflect the stated priority, the organization is not actually focused.
