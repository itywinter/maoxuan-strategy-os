# Experiment Design

## Purpose

Turn an important assumption into a real-world test that can change a decision.

## Distinguish

- demo — shows something can be displayed
- proof of concept — shows technical feasibility
- pilot — works in a real operational context
- experiment — tests a specific hypothesis

## Design

Define before launch:

- decision to inform
- hypothesis and alternative
- target/context
- baseline
- intervention
- evidence sources
- technical / operational / economic / commercial metrics
- duration
- owner
- success / modify / stop / scale thresholds
- confounders

Prefer the smallest **valid** test, not the smallest possible test. An underpowered test can create a false negative.

## Commercial commitment

Praise, meetings, and usage are weaker evidence than budget, paid extension, procurement action, or repeated purchase.

## After success

A successful pilot does not prove repeatability or scalability. Move one rung at a time on the validation ladder.
