# Decision Thresholds

Define before material action what outcomes imply:

- **Continue** — evidence supports more investment
- **Modify** — direction may be valid but approach needs change
- **Stop** — evidence materially weakens the thesis or economics
- **Scale** — evidence supports a larger commitment
- **Pause** — an external condition prevents a valid decision

Thresholds may be quantitative, behavioral, structural, or strategic. Important decisions often require several conditions; one attractive metric should not hide a failure elsewhere.

## Hierarchy

Safety/legal/survival kill criteria may override strategic, commercial, economic, or technical success.

## Governance

Do not retrospectively change thresholds to re-label an observed result. If the next cycle needs better criteria, record the change and apply it prospectively.
