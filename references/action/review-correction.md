# Review & Correction

## Purpose

Make results change decisions rather than merely generate reports.

## Review questions

- What did we expect?
- What happened?
- What evidence confirms, weakens, contradicts, or expands the model?
- Did stage, primary contradiction, dominant aspect, or hard constraints change?
- What is the correct correction level: execution, tactical, strategic, model?

## Threshold integrity

Do not move goalposts after results are known. If criteria must change, record the reason and apply the new criteria prospectively.

## Anti-sunk-cost question

**If we had not already invested anything, would we invest the next unit of resource today?**

## Current-state refresh

Before turning historical lessons into a new strategy, update objective, actors, resources, constraints, market, capabilities, and stage.

## Decision log

For material decisions record date, evidence, assumptions, decision, thresholds, owner, and review trigger so future reconstruction does not depend on memory.
