# Gross-to-Net Economics

Headline upside is not decision value.

Translate where material:

**Gross benefit − implementation cost − transition cost − displacement/cannibalization − financing/working capital − downside/risk adjustment = net decision value**.

Also separate accounting presentation from decision-relevant cash economics. Allocated overhead that remains after a shutdown is not an avoidable saving; a short-term revenue decline caused by ratable subscription recognition may not imply value destruction.
