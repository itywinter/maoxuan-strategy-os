# Incentive Analysis

## Purpose

Adjust the weight of advice and evidence when sources benefit differently from possible outcomes.

Ask of important sources:

- What expertise do they have?
- What do they directly observe?
- Are they independent?
- What result improves their compensation, status, budget, control, or legacy?

Examples:

- banker paid mainly if a deal closes
- salesperson rewarded for bookings
- product leader benefits from a six-month build programme
- CEO publicly tied identity to a strategic bet

Incentive conflict does not make information false. It lowers independence and increases the need for corroboration.
