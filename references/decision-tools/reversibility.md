# Reversibility & Option Value

Use when choices differ materially in how hard they are to undo.

## One-way versus two-way doors

- Reversible decisions justify faster action with lower evidence burden.
- Irreversible or highly costly decisions require stronger evidence, scenario analysis, and explicit downside tolerance.

## Option value

When long-run expected value is similar, prefer a path that preserves future choices, produces information, and limits irreversible downside—unless delay itself destroys meaningful value.

## Staged commitment

Consider pilots, milestones, phased capital, conditions precedent, limited exclusivity, or other structures that convert one-way commitments into learnable stages.
