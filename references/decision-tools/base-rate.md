# Base Rate / Outside View

Use when forecasting high-uncertainty outcomes such as M&A integration, transformation timelines, startup success, project overruns, or adoption.

Compare:

- **Inside view:** why this specific case may succeed/fail
- **Outside view:** what usually happens in comparable cases

Base rates calibrate; they do not mechanically replace specific evidence. If management argues “we are different”, ask what observable capability or condition justifies the departure from the base rate.
