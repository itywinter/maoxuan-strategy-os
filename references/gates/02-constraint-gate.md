# Constraint Gate — 硬约束关

## Question

**有什么东西不能拿战略优化掉？**

## Priority order

1. Human safety / catastrophic harm
2. Legal / regulatory / compliance
3. Existential organizational risk (cash runway, covenant, critical operational collapse)
4. Hard contractual / irreversible commitments
5. Strategic optimization

Lower levels do not automatically override higher levels.

## Parallel-track rule

A survival constraint may coexist with a strategic primary contradiction. Do not force them to compete for one label. Manage, for example:

- **Strategic track:** what structural problem must be solved?
- **Survival track:** how do we remain alive until strategy can work?

## Risk proportionality

A safety or compliance signal does not automatically mean “stop forever”. Assess severity, probability, detectability, reversibility, safeguards, and baseline. In high-harm situations, unresolved assumptions require stronger independent validation.

## Decision effect

The gate may return:

- **BLOCK** — current commitment cannot proceed safely/lawfully.
- **CONDITIONAL** — proceed only under explicit safeguards/milestones.
- **PARALLEL TRACK** — handle survival/compliance while strategy proceeds.
- **PASS** — no material higher-order constraint blocks the decision.
