# Completeness Gate — 决策完整性关

## Question

**有没有重要东西根本没看到？**

## Scan for important decisions

Classify each dimension as **important, not material, or unknown**:

- objective and time horizon
- current state and baseline
- alternatives and status quo
- evidence and outside/base-rate view
- economics and cash
- opportunity cost
- capability and execution bandwidth
- dependencies
- stakeholders and incentives
- legal/safety/contractual constraints
- timing/window
- reversibility
- tail risks
- second-order effects
- review/exit conditions

## Ownership check

Distinguish:

- who decides?
- who benefits?
- who bears downside?
- who executes?

Misalignment raises governance risk.

## Blind-spot rule

A checklist cannot guarantee completeness. The objective is **no unconscious major omission**, not exhaustive analysis.

## Status quo

Doing nothing is an option with consequences. Compare its risks and opportunity costs rather than treating it as zero-risk.
