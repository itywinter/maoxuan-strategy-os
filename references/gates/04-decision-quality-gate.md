# Decision Quality Gate — 决策质量关

## Question

**这个决定需要多高的证据标准？**

## Burden of proof

Analysis depth should increase with:

**Consequence × Irreversibility × Uncertainty**.

A reversible pilot can tolerate more uncertainty than a large acquisition, shutdown, multi-year exclusivity agreement, or safety-critical release.

## Check

- **Base rate / outside view:** what usually happens in comparable cases?
- **Inside view:** what specific evidence makes this case different?
- **Gross-to-net economics:** subtract implementation, transition, cannibalization, opportunity cost, and risk.
- **Thesis-critical assumptions:** which assumption, if false, destroys the core logic?
- **Incentives:** who benefits if the decision goes through?
- **One-way door:** can we retreat cheaply if wrong?
- **Option value:** can we stage the commitment and learn first?
- **Value of information:** which unknown must be resolved before commitment and which can be learned later?

## Deadline rule

A real deadline changes time available; it does not make unknown facts true. Do not lower evidence standards merely because a counterparty creates urgency.

## Decision structure

Do not accept false binaries. Consider price, timing, scope, staged investment, performance conditions, earn-outs, pilots, termination rights, or other structures that change risk allocation.
