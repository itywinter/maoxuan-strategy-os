# Reality Gate — 求真关

## Question

**事情到底是什么样？**

## Use when

Facts, causes, metrics, stakeholder stories, or evidence quality are unclear.

## Workflow

1. Define the observable phenomenon without inserting cause.
2. Separate facts, claims, interpretations, hypotheses, and unknowns.
3. Decompose material aggregate metrics before causal attribution.
4. Identify the baseline or comparator for claims of improvement, decline, safety, or value.
5. Generate credible competing hypotheses; do not keep only the user's preferred explanation.
6. Prioritize decision-critical unknowns (P0), high-value unknowns (P1), and optional unknowns (P2).
7. Prefer discriminating evidence that can eliminate multiple hypotheses.
8. Assess source independence and incentives.
9. Exit when critical facts are verified or reasonably bounded and remaining uncertainty is explicit.

## Rules

- Investigation is uncertainty reduction, not information accumulation.
- Do not ask twenty questions when three can separate the leading hypotheses.
- A strong statement by a senior person remains a statement unless direct evidence supports it.
- A correct metric can still be misleading if its denominator, scope, or decomposition is wrong.

## Output

Usually show only what helps the user:

- confirmed facts
- what is interpretation/hypothesis
- critical unknowns
- leading explanations and evidence status
- what can and cannot yet be concluded
