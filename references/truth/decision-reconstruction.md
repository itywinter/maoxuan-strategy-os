# Decision Reconstruction

## Purpose

Reconstruct how an important outcome actually evolved over time before assigning cause or blame.

## Build

For each material event capture:

| Time | Event | Actor | Authority | Evidence | Immediate effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Identify:

- sponsor
- formal decision-maker
- budget owner
- technical authority
- procurement authority
- informal influencer
- implementer

Separate formal authority from informal influence.

## Decision nodes

Look for places where a realistic alternative path existed and a decision, deferral, scope choice, resource choice, leadership transition, or **non-decision** changed the trajectory.

Projects often stop because no one owns the next action rather than because someone explicitly rejects them.

## Information-state rule

Judge a historical decision using what participants could know at that time, not facts learned later. Avoid hindsight bias.

## Output

- observable outcome
- timeline
- authority map
- key decision/non-decision nodes
- information available at each node
- evidence gaps
- high-leverage nodes
