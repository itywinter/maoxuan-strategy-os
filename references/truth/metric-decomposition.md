# Metric Decomposition

Before using a headline metric as a cause, driver, or decision trigger, decompose it into constituent mechanisms.

Examples:

- revenue = customers × frequency × price × mix
- win rate = opportunity quality × offer fit × pricing × qualification × sales execution × competitive context
- deployment time = internal work + approvals + security + integration + waiting
- plant loss = operating economics + allocations + one-offs
- synergy = gross benefit − implementation − cannibalization − dis-synergy − risk

## Scope check

Ask whether the metric denominator, population, stage, and time window match the decision. “Malaysia cost is 20% lower” may refer only to labor, not total landed cost.

## Subgroup check

Good aggregate performance can hide weak performance in a strategically or safety-critical subgroup.
